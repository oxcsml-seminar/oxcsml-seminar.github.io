OxCSML Seminar Series

The OxCSML Seminar is a research seminar series hosted by the Department of Statistics at the University of Oxford. We invite external speakers to present recent work in statistics, machine learning and related areas.

The series provides an opportunity for researchers and students to learn about current research and engage in discussion with visiting speakers. All members of the research community are welcome to attend.

Researchers and students from other departments and institutions are very welcome, including Master's students. No affiliation with the Department of Statistics is required.

To suggest a speaker or topic, or to offer to give a talk, please contact one of the organisers ↗.
